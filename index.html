OUR OFFERINGS

SETTING

A knife, left by the murderer and stabbed into the wall. There are no fingerprints on it, but the blood is Katherine's. The height it is at in the wall would suggest the culprit is someone of average height.

You have formally been invited to the beautiful home of the illustrious Sir Christensen. It is to be be a night of great merriment, with many options for both cuisine and entertainment.

Of course, this is not just any party. Sir Christensen’s wife, the late Katherine, has passed under mysterious circumstances. Your magnanimous host believes the culprit to be among your fellow guests.

As Sir Christensen’s most erudite friend, he is depending on you to avenge his wife. And if anything were to happen to him, well, you’d have to have to resolve that also.

Step into a night of of joy and thrilling spectacle—but be sure to keep your head on your shoulders.

COMPANIONS

Your fellow guests are not yet aware of your true intentions. Keeping your suspicions and background a secret will be your greatest protection from retaliation. Of course, the murderer will be doing the same.

Some of the other innocent guests may have clues for you that will lead you to the correct answer. There may also be hints left throughout Sir Christensen’s home. Finding them and discerning which are relevant and have been faked will prevent the night from going to waste.

In order to attend, you will need to fill out the invite form located below. All attendees must fill it out separately to ensure that everyone is appropriately accommodated for. There is a total limit of ten guests for the night.

The night will start with dinner. Several actors will be hidden among the guests from the beginning. During dinner, further instruction will be given and any major events will only occur with all guests in the same room.

After dinner the night will be spent investigating and speaking with your fellow guests. You will have until midnight to come to a conclusion.

The murderer will try to throw you off their track, so watch for red herrings and suspicious behavior.

Do not trust easily.

Sir Christensen's fingerprints, found near Katherine's body. Many of them were found near her.

After dinner, appetizers and refreshments will continue to be available until the end of the night or until supply runs out.

ENTERTAINMENT

In order to prepare for the party, Sir Christensen has generously prepared a trial run for you. He has hidden clues throughout this site so that your detective skills will be up to snuff. For your benefit, he has enlisted the help of other guests who will be present.

If you are certain of your suspicions, you may select the person you believe to be culpable.
